# AI Assistant Guide — Rental One

This repository is the single source of truth for the Rental One brand. Rental One is an operating company of Cotton Co (`haydenclayne-ctrl/cotton-co`), under High Cotton Capital. It has its own distinct identity — never mix in Cotton Co black/gold, HCC marks, or DCR Ranch assets.

## Rules

1. **Never invent brand facts.** Colors and type come from `visual-identity/`; logo rules from `brand-guidelines/logo.md`; messaging from `brand-guidelines/messaging.md`. If a file doesn't cover it, say so.
2. **The logo is fixed.** The framed "RENTAL / 19 ONE 96" lockup is the logo — never redraw, retype, restyle, recolor, or rearrange it. `assets/logo/` holds faithful vector reproductions for layout use; production artwork comes from master files.
3. **Visuals**: Rental One Navy `#041E42` dominates (~55% of any layout), white co-primary, Signal Orange `#F26A21` at ≤5% and never touching the logo. Display type is condensed industrial ALL CAPS (Industry / United Sans / Tungsten class); body is Archivo; data/labels are IBM Plex Mono.
4. **Feel**: premium industrial — strong, clean, modern, dependable, timeless. Never cheap, cartoonish, generic, playful, rustic, or busy. Think mission support, not equipment rental.
5. **Voice**: concise, confident, concrete. Lead with outcomes, active voice, specifics over superlatives. No hype, no exclamation points, no buzzwords.
6. **Endorsement**: "A Cotton Co Company" in small type on corporate materials only — never locked into the logo, never inside its clear space.
7. Naming: kebab-case folders, markdown-first. Never delete history — archive it.
