# Applications

How the identity translates to the places it shows up most. Default everywhere: navy field, white logo, more white space than feels necessary.

## Stationery

- **Business cards** — 3.5 × 2 in, 16 pt uncoated, navy as PMS 534. Front: logo centered on navy, nothing else. Back: white, left-aligned name/role/contact, one Signal Orange rule.
- **Letterhead** — logo top-left at 1.5 in; "A Cotton Co Company" in the footer.

## Fleet & equipment

- **Trucks & trailers** — full navy wrap or navy panel on white cab. Logo ≥⅓ of panel height; URL + phone in display caps below. Rear doors: ONE box mark + URL.
- **Containers & modulars** — navy end-panels, white one-color logo, unit ID stenciled (Plex Mono style) below. Long sides carry the wordmark once, never repeated.
- **Equipment** — white one-color decal on OEM paint where wraps aren't practical; plate-style asset tag (`R1-####`).

## Jobsite & PPE

- **Hard hats** — white or navy shells; ONE box decal front-center, 2 in. No full lockup (fails at helmet curvature).
- **Safety vests** — hi-vis per ANSI class; safety trumps brand color. Logo one-color navy left chest; "RENTAL ONE" display caps across back yoke.
- **Site signage** — navy panels, white logo; Signal Orange reserved for safety/wayfinding. Gate signs: logo + one line only ("Project Support On Site.").

## Apparel

Uniform, not merch. Navy or white only — no camo, no novelty colorways.

- **Polos** — navy body, white embroidered ONE box left chest (2.5 in). Full-lockup embroidery only on jackets ≥3 in.
- **T-shirts** — full lockup center chest (9 in) or small left chest; back may carry the wordmark large across shoulders.
- **Hats** — navy structured cap, white ONE box front panel. No side embroidery, no contrast stitching.

## Digital

- **Email signature** — HTML-safe Arial: name in navy caps, role in Steel Blue letterspaced caps, one 40 px orange rule, "Rental One · A Cotton Co Company," phone + URL. Logo optional at 120 px.
- **Social** — ONE box on navy for avatars at all sizes; full lockup reserved for banners.
- **Website** — navy headers/heroes, white content sections, Signal Orange CTAs only.
- **Presentations & proposals** — navy cover with logo (duotone photography allowed), white body slides, keyline-framed figures, mono plate footers (project · date · page).

## Trade shows

Navy environments with the logo lit in white — never white booths with navy accents. Blueprint grid on large walls at low opacity. One message per wall face in display caps. Counters carry the ONE box; giveaways follow apparel rules.
