# Typography

Three roles, three faces. Never more than these three families in one composition.

## Faces

| Role | Face | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | **Industry** (Fort Foundry) | Bold, Black | Always ALL CAPS. Octagonal industrial forms that echo the logo without imitating it. Alternates: United Sans, Tungsten. Licensed (Adobe Fonts / MyFonts). Never below 18 px, never for paragraphs. |
| Text | **Archivo** (Google Fonts) | Regular–Bold | Body copy, UI, documents. Never letterspaced. Digital alternate: Roboto. |
| Data | **IBM Plex Mono** | Regular, Medium | Spec tables, equipment IDs, dates, part numbers, eyebrow labels. |

**Web-safe fallbacks** (email, Office templates only): display/text → Arial, Helvetica (bold + all caps + ~2% tracking approximates display); data → Consolas/Menlo.
