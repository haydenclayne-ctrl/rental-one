# Rental One — Brand Overview

Recorded 2026-07-20 from the founder's brand brief.

## What Rental One is

Rental One provides the equipment, workforce, logistics, and temporary infrastructure that keep projects moving — from mobilization through project completion. It is a **project support partner**, not a generic equipment rental company. Customers rely on Rental One because it solves operational challenges, not simply because it rents equipment.

## Position in the family

High Cotton Capital (parent platform) → Cotton Co (operating group) → **Rental One** (execution company). Rental One maintains its own distinct identity while feeling like it belongs to a premium family of companies. The relationship is expressed through endorsement ("A Cotton Co Company"), never co-branding.

## Capabilities

**Equipment & temporary infrastructure** — equipment rentals · temporary infrastructure · portable offices & modular facilities · climate-controlled storage containers · temporary power & generators · temporary lighting · material handling · delivery, setup & demobilization

**Labor & project support** — skilled labor supply · general labor · workforce support · project staffing · site logistics

**FF&E & relocation** — FF&E installation · furniture moving & relocation · office relocations · healthcare and educational facility moves · warehousing & asset management

## Markets served

Commercial construction · industrial · manufacturing · general contractors · government agencies · healthcare · education · corporate facilities

## Personality

**Is:** strong, modern, clean, industrial, premium, dependable, professional, bold, confident, timeless.

**Is never:** cheap, cartoonish, generic, overly playful, overly rustic, trend-driven, busy or cluttered.

The identity communicates: reliability, preparedness, precision, professionalism, confidence, operational excellence, execution, partnership. Think less "equipment rental," more "mission support."
