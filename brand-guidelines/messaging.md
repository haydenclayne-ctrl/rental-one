# Messaging

## Tagline

**Primary: "One Source. Every Phase."** — owns the name, states the position, scales from a truck door to a proposal cover.

Campaign alternates (one per composition, never stacked):
- "Mobilize. Execute. Deliver." — recruiting and culture
- "Built to Keep Projects Moving." — fleet and web hero
- "Project Support, Delivered." — service-line collateral

Tagline sits outside the logo's clear space, display caps, ≤25% of logo width. Never locked permanently into the logo artwork.

## Tone of voice

Concise, confident, concrete.

- **Lead with the outcome** — "Power on site in 48 hours," not "We offer generator solutions"
- **Short sentences, active voice** — we deliver, we install, we stand behind
- **Specifics over superlatives** — counts, timelines, certifications beat "best-in-class"
- **Plain trade language** — talk like the jobsite, write like the contract

Never: hype, exclamation points, buzzwords ("synergy," "solutions-oriented"), hedging, humor at the trade's expense. The brand never shouts.

## Elevator pitch

"Rental One is a project support company. Equipment, temporary infrastructure, labor, logistics, and FF&E — one partner from mobilization to completion. Contractors and agencies call us because we solve operational problems, not just fill rental orders."

## Boilerplate

**Short (25 words):** Rental One is a project support company providing equipment, temporary infrastructure, labor, logistics, and FF&E services from mobilization through completion. A Cotton Co company.

**Standard (60 words):** Rental One provides the equipment, workforce, and temporary infrastructure that keep projects moving. Serving commercial construction, industrial, government, healthcare, education, and corporate clients, we deliver equipment rentals, portable offices, temporary power, skilled labor, site logistics, and FF&E installation — one accountable partner from mobilization through completion. Rental One is an operating company of Cotton Co, a High Cotton Capital platform.
