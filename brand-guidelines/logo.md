# Logo Standards

The Rental One logo is a framed industrial lockup: the RENTAL wordmark in blocky octagonal capitals, the ONE box breaking the bottom keyline, and the establishing date split "19 / 96" at either side, with the ® mark. It reads like a stamped equipment plate — permanent, load-rated, certified.

**The logo is fixed.** Never redraw, retype, auto-trace, or reinterpret it. `../assets/logo/` contains the owner-provided logo files — the only approved artwork: `r1-logo.png` (white on navy) and `r1-logo-2.png` (navy on light).

## Anatomy

- **Keyline frame** — the outer border; part of the logo, never removed
- **Wordmark** — RENTAL in octagonal slab capitals
- **ONE box** — the framed ONE that intentionally breaks the bottom keyline
- **Est. dates** — "19" and "96" flanking the ONE box
- **® mark** — retained at all reproducible sizes

## Approved versions

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| White on Rental One Navy | Default — the brand's home state |
| Navy on white | Light surfaces |
| One-color white | Embroidery, etching, spot print on dark |
| One-color black | Fax, engraving, newsprint |
| ONE box (secondary mark) | Avatars, favicons, hard-hat decals, small embroidery — anywhere the full lockup falls below minimum size |

Pick whichever gives maximum contrast; there is no middle ground. No gradients, outlines, extra colors, or partial lockups other than the ONE box.

## Clear space & minimum size

- **Clear space:** 1X on all sides, where X = cap height of "ONE" in the box. Nothing enters it — type, imagery, other logos, page edges.
- **Minimum size:** print 1.25 in / 32 mm wide · digital 120 px wide · embroidery 2.5 in. Below these, use the ONE box mark (min 0.5 in / 48 px).

## Backgrounds

**Yes:** Rental One Navy, Midnight, white, black; photography with a 40–60% navy scrim behind the logo zone; high-contrast physical materials.

**No:** mid-tones, gradients, patterns, busy photography without a scrim, any orange or accent-colored background.

## Never

Stretch, condense, rotate, recolor, add effects/shadows/outlines, rearrange elements, remove the keyline or dates, place on low contrast or busy backgrounds, type "Rental One" in display type as a substitute logo, place the logo inside a containing shape, or animate it beyond a simple fade.

## Endorsed lockup

For proposals, government submittals, and corporate documents: full logo with "A COTTON CO COMPANY" set beneath in letterspaced caps (secondary typeface) at ~40% of logo width — outside the clear space, never inside the artwork.
